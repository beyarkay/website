Nice things from lighthaven:

- plentiful snacks, but only healthy ones and only kept a long walking distance
  away. also ideally single-servings, no binging
- Lots of green, even if it's fake plants
  - fake vines or little fake pot plants in crannies
  - also real things, but this can get tricky
- big comfy couches with monitors on hospital arms
- lots of rugs and carpets and low-to-the-floor seating
- heaters on the ceiling
- chargers _everywhere_
- lots of shaded outdoor spots
- Lots of Astra turf and seated areas
- Kinda confusing arrangement of buildings
- Lots of attention to lighting.
  - Massive luminators that are incredibly bright
  - Very few ceiling lights, many many soft lights
  - lots of decorative lights, like fairy lights in a corner or nook, or
    hanging lanterns
- lots of little fidget toys
- lots of wooden balance things
- air purifiers
- seating:
  - lots of egg/papasan/hanging chairs
- gas fire pits for warmth and something nice to sit round
- blackout curtains
- privacy films
- window screens!
- lots of things in the most convenient place, not the "normal" place.
- lots of whiteboards on the walls!
- lots of nooks and blocked line-of-sight so people feel cosy and hidden from
  the world
- the weird laptop padded things
- nicely setup bathrooms with toilet spray & nice pump-action hand soap storage
  space for lots of backup supplies.
