Going to a bar:

- Warm up socially beforehand:
  - call a friend/family on the way to the bar
  - Talk to strangers in shops or on the road, ask what they're doing tonight
    and where they're going
  - Get the first hello out the way immediately, as soon as you enter the
    bar
- Getting some friends
  - make friends with the closest group of girls who are out on a girls
    night, saying that your friends stood you up / are all with their
    girlfriends, and you're just looking to meet some girls.
- Attitude
  - Goal should be to sharpening your social skills, not to sleep with
    someone or to hook up. Also to have fun, eventually.
  - I want the skill to be able to meet new people at any bar in any city, this
    skill will take time to develop
