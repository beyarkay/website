Sometimes I see formulaic things going viral on tpot, so here's a catalogue of
them. These are all things where I think "Oh I could have written that" but I
didn't, but maybe I can write them next time. I'm making this public, because
generally virality on tpot is a good indicator of high-quality content, and I'd
like to see more high-quality content, even if I might be competing for
creating that high-quality content.

# Blogpost explaining a research paper

For example:

- [@cneuralnetwork][1] made a [tweet][2] linking their [blogpost][3] explaining
  DeepSeek r1 on the 25th Jan 2025, a few days after release.
  - Posted: `6:50 PM · Jan 25, 2025`
  - Appeared on timeline: `21:18 25 Jan 2025`
  - 216.7K Views
  - 3.8K Likes
  - 4.4K Bookmarks
  - Followers gained on the 26th (according to social blade): +659 (vs +122 daily average)

These seem pretty easy to replicate. Every time a paper comes out that seems
interesting but just slightly too math-y or long to read through it all, give a
deep dive, explain the math, link the blogpost.
