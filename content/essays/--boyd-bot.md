A few years ago I made a simple telegram bot to automate various miscellaneous
things in my life. It's been one of the most impactful things I've ever done.
Here's what it does, and why you should consider writing your own.

- Exercise tracking
- Ideas logging
- Launchpad for other ideas/websites.
- Include a graph (maybe regularly updated?) of the embeddings of the ideas
  alongside some common queries or topics like "robotics" or "websites" to give
  people an idea of the clustering.
