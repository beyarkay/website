title: Which way will you fail?
tags: [needs-writing, failure, two-choices]
---
unlisted: true

Sometimes, it's really really tricky to be correct, and you're dramatically
more likely to overshoot or undershoot. But _very_ often, you can basically
guarantee the _direction_ of your failure: you can take actions to almost
ensure that you'll either 1. be correct or overshoot, or 2. be correct or
undershoot.

This is a powerful idea, because often you have a preference about whether you
overshoot/undershoot, and sometimes it's a _very_ strong preference. For
example, I want people to SHA256 more predictions, to allow people to make
predictions and then reveal that they knew a thing, without giving the game
away. But wait! people could just SHA256 _every_ variant of a prediction and
then just reveal the one they know to be true. That's fair critique, but I'd
_significantly_ prefer more people SHA256'ing their predictions and some
fraction of them be defectors who SHA256 everything, than the current state of
the world where nobody predicts anything.
