The thinkish is too damn high. Claude Opus 4.8 regularly conjures
session-specific jargon without explanation to the user, and proceeds to use
it. This is a surprising lack of Theory-of-Mind from an LLM trained to be
helpful.

AKA: The pitch for pedagogy as an AI safety pretraining requirement.

- Current AIs are bad at explaining things
- as AIs get superhuman, explaining things will become more important
- Good explanations is plausibly a reasonably-easy thing to design an RL
  environment around.
