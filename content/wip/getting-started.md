Sometimes, I get asked how to get started with _thing_, where _thing_ is some
topic that I'm not a novice about. Often I struggle to describe how I got
started, because it's inevitably some weird mix of blog posts, people, and
talks that I've read/watched over a long period of time. This is my attempt at
cataloguing these things so that I can have a place to point people towards,
and will also serve the purpose of being a log of high-quality learning
material that I've found useful. In the future I expect pointing an LLM at a
list of teaching material that I've found useful, will in and of itself be
useful.

## Personal Blogs/Websites

Some people seem to produce content so aligned with my interests that I'm happy
to recommend whatever they're doing, regardless of the content that they're
discussing.

- [Andrej Karpathy's blog](https://karpathy.github.io): well written,
  interesting, deep technical dives into AI/ML topics. Loads of technical
  details and he's not afraid to say it like it is ("We're not using biases
  because meh."). Very easy to read, he
  clearly explains topics without resorting to jargon.
