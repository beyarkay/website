So I agree with the concept of an `/ideas` page as a means to finding people
who you can collaborate with (read the [about-ideas-now manifesto][1]), but it
also seems like the perfect place for people to poach good ideas from. So
I'll put some moonshot ideas down here. Things that I don't think are
practical today (or won't be practical for me in the next ~10 years). Things
which I wouldn't be super upset about if people stole them. I'll also link to
further writing, if I've written further.

- [Octopus training][/wip/octos] and pouring dramatically more money into non-human animal
  intelligence training. Think puppy school, but with funding measured in
  hundreds of millions, not tens of thousands.
- Dramatic redesign of education

If you're interested in my space of ideas, have a look at my write-up of
[`boyd-bot`][2], a telegram bot I wrote to help track a number of things about my
life, including logging ideas to a private `ideas.yaml` file.
